AetherFrame sharing test: how to install
========================================

This is a test build of AetherFrame with sharing turned on. It is not a release. Use it only for
the sharing test you were invited to, and remove it afterwards.

What you need
-------------
- FFXIV on Windows with Dalamud (XIVLauncher). Mac and Linux, including Wine and Proton, aren't
  supported yet: the key that proves your character is yours is protected by Windows.
- Your character's Lodestone page. Send its address to the person who invited you before you
  start. The test's server only accepts the characters on its list, and can add yours only once it
  has the number at the end of that address.

1. Unpack
---------
Unzip this kit somewhere it can stay, for example C:\AetherFrame test\. You'll get a folder named
AetherFrame holding three files: AetherFrame.dll, AetherFrame.json and AetherFrame.deps.json.
SHA256SUMS.txt lists their checksums if you want to check them.

2. Turn off any other AetherFrame
---------------------------------
If you installed AetherFrame from a plugin repository, open /xlplugins, find AetherFrame under
Installed Plugins, and disable it. Two copies running at once would share the same Plates.

3. Add the test build to Dalamud
--------------------------------
1. Type /xlsettings in chat and open the Experimental tab.
2. Under Dev Plugin Locations, paste the full path of the DLL itself, for example
   C:\AetherFrame test\AetherFrame\AetherFrame.dll, and press the + button. Make sure its box is
   ticked.
3. Press Save and Close.
4. Type /xlplugins, open Dev Tools, then Installed Dev Plugins, and enable AetherFrame.
5. Type /af version. It should say "network preview".

Your Plates, Templates and settings are the same ones any AetherFrame on this PC uses.

4. Share your Plate
-------------------
1. Type /af to open My Plates, then press Sharing.
2. Read what sharing means, tick the box, and turn sharing on for the character you're logged in as.
3. AetherFrame shows a one-time code. Sign in to the Lodestone, open your character's Character
   Profile in its settings, paste the code anywhere in it, and save.
4. Paste your Lodestone page's address into AetherFrame and press Check. When it passes, you can
   delete the code from your profile.
5. AetherFrame shows your Active Plate exactly as others will see it. Press Share this Plate.
   From then on, saving your Active Plate updates what others see.

5. View other players' Plates
-----------------------------
Once your character shares, right-click another tester's character in game (in the world, the
party list, the friend list or chat) and choose View AetherFrame Plate. You can also search for
them by full name and World: My Plates, Sharing, then Find a player's Plate.

You can only see players who share too. You can hide a player's Plate on your PC, or report one.

6. Stop sharing, and remove the test build
------------------------------------------
- To stop sharing, open My Plates, Sharing, and turn sharing off. The server then deletes your
  shared Plate and your character's link to it.
- To remove the test build, turn sharing off first. Then untick or remove its line in
  /xlsettings, Experimental, Dev Plugin Locations, and delete the folder you unzipped. Your
  Plates stay where they are.

What the server keeps
---------------------
For each character that shares: its Lodestone id, name and World, a key that proves the character
is yours, and your latest shared Plate. It sees the network address of anyone who shares or views,
and doesn't store it. It keeps no record of who viewed whom. Your Plates themselves stay on your
PC: the server only has the picture you shared.

If something goes wrong
-----------------------
Tell the person who invited you what you did and what you saw. If they ask for your log, type
/xllog, filter by AetherFrame, and copy those lines. AetherFrame's log lines never include names,
Worlds, codes or keys.
